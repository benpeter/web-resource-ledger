# Decisions

- New page `site/content/use-cases/takedown-evidence.md` ("Evidence for
  DMCA and DSA Takedowns"), for both sides of a takedown: the rights holder
  who captures before sending a notice, and the person whose content was
  removed. It quotes 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3), (f) and (g) and DSA Articles 16,
  17, 19 to 21 from primary texts (Cornell LII, the Copyright Office, the
  Official Journal text from the EU Publications Office, since EUR-Lex
  answered scripts with an empty 202). The quote list with URLs is kept
  with the drafts, not in the repo.
- WRL facts in the page were checked against the code and docs: DigiCert
  as the RFC 3161 authority (`TSA_URL`), 10 schedules, hourly or slower, the
  dashboard's Changed/Unchanged badges, 200 URLs in the list form, 10
  captures a minute, 50 qualified timestamps a month free then €0.10, free
  tier 200 captures and 10 GB.
- The page is about 1,750 words, longer than the other use-case pages. Kept:
  the notice elements are quoted from the statutes, and cutting them would
  turn the page into paraphrase.
- Two FAQ entries on the landing page, with matching FAQPage JSON-LD. A
  third draft ("how long infringing content stayed online") repeated the
  existing trademark entry's schedule answer and was left out.
- Claims the sub-agent could not source were left out: how fast providers
  act after a notice, how platforms handle trademark complaints outside the
  EU, and what evidence a court accepts (the page points to Legal Evidence).
